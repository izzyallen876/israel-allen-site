lab

Three small things in real 3D, written from scratch in WebGL with no libraries, and one tiny neural network you can train yourself. Drag to orbit. Tab to a scene and use the arrow keys if you prefer the keyboard.

SPECIMEN_01

Heat relief

A thermal-style street image, lifted into 3D by how hot each pixel is, so the people stand up out of the road. Click to send a pulse through it. Double-click to reset the view.

drag to orbit

SPECIMEN_02

Pixel monolith

Every pixel of a pixelated portrait becomes a bar, and the darker the pixel, the taller the bar. Move across it and the bars rise to meet you. Click for a shockwave.

drag to orbit

SPECIMEN_03

The crystal, unrendered

A cousin of the crystal on the home page, this time as real geometry over a scrolling grid. Change how many sides it has and watch the reflection follow.

drag to orbit

SPECIMEN_04

Train a tiny neural network

A small network written from scratch in JavaScript, with no libraries. Click the board to add points of two colours, or load a shape, then press Train and watch it bend a boundary between them. This is a toy for seeing how a classifier learns. It is not part of my research.

Load a shape to begin.

Keyboard: tab to a scene, then use the arrow keys to orbit, + and − to zoom and 0 to reset. The two images are shown as artwork, and their status is explained on the credits page.
